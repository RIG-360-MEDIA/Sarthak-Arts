Part two — the depth

What I studied, and what it changes.

You were right — the earlier version lacked soul. A devotional Vastu brand with consultations, astrology and social should feel sacred first, and beautiful second. Below: the exemplars I studied, the essentials our current build was missing, and the uniquely-ours moves that make this the only site of its kind.

The audit

What was missing.

Two lists. Left: e-commerce essentials our current build simply doesn't have — table stakes for premium D2C. Right: the moves that are uniquely ours to make, because we're a direction-first devotional brand with consultations and astrology built in.

Standard e-commerce essentials — missing

  • Wishlist (guest, no forced signup)
  • Quick view on card hover
  • "Complete your altar" cross-sell on PDP
  • Trust badges — payments, secure, returns
  • Photo reviews — 6× more influential than text
  • Sticky mobile Add-to-cart — +8–12% ATC lift
  • Multiple product images + zoom
  • 360° / video product views
  • Cart drawer, not a page navigation
  • Search autocomplete + suggestions
  • Recently viewed strip
  • Product variants — finish / patina / size
  • Visual order tracking — timeline, not a word
  • Customer accounts — currently guest-only
  • Gift wrap option
  • Loyalty / referral — a "Bhakta Circle"
  • Micro-interactions + considered empty states

Sarthak Arts–unique — never attempted

  • Live Choghadiya / Panchang woven into the header — nobody in devotional D2C does this properly. Signature move.
  • Direction wheel as a yantra, not a filter — each cardinal point a page: element, deity, mantra, and the pieces made for it.
  • Ritual guidance on every PDP — Sankalpa → Cleansing → Placement → First Puja.
  • Certificate of composition as an illuminated scroll — currently a plain PDF; should be an heirloom.
  • Consultation woven through the journey — floating "Ask an astrologer" chip on PDP; post-purchase consecration reading.
  • Birth-chart-linked recommendations — after an astrology session, product picks tune to the chart.
  • Home audit as concierge (Warby Parker–style branching), not a flat form.
  • "Bhaktas' homes" gallery — real customer altars with the pieces in place.
  • Live-from-the-workshop feed — dispatches, not a static Instagram grid.
  • Ritual calendar — next festival + the pieces relevant to it.
  • Deity-specific placement guidance on murti pages.
  • Ambient sacred motion — mandala, diya, incense — considered, never gaudy.
  • Devanagari as ornament — mantras, deity names, tithi in script.
  • Aarti-time reminders — subtle notice at sandhya kaal.

Motion & ritual

  • The mandala breathes. A large mandala rotates once every 90 seconds. It's ambient, not busy.
  • Diyas flicker. Two lit diyas frame the hero, flames trembling on their own rhythms.
  • Gold falls. Fine gold particles descend slowly — like the flame's sparks or ceremony rice.
  • Petals drift. A few marigold and rose petals fall through the frame at irregular intervals.
  • Incense curls. Small columns of soft-white smoke rise on either side.
  • The Om breathes. The central glyph pulses with a slow warm glow — 4-second cycle, like a mantra chant.
  • Sacred pauses. Full-bleed dark sections mark the moments that matter — hero, consultant, certificate reveal.
  • Reduced motion honored. Every animation collapses cleanly under prefers-reduced-motion.

How the rebuild unfolds.

Sign this off first. I don't touch code until you do. Then, in order:

01

Sacred foundations

Palette, type, motion primitives (mandala, particles, flame, glow), Devanagari font stack, ornaments, small components. Everything below is built from these.

02

Storefront — the sanctum

Rebuild home with the animated hero, live panchang, mantra marquee. The direction shrine (yantra with deity-per-cardinal). Ritual-guide PDP with wishlist, quick view, cross-sell, sticky mobile ATC, photo reviews. Quiz-as-concierge home audit.

03

The illuminated certificate

Redesign the composition PDF as an illuminated scroll — deity, direction, weights, consecration date. Something worth framing.

04

Consultant + astrologer sanctuary

Booking as an editorial moment. Birth-chart-linked recommendations. "Ask" chips on PDP. Post-purchase consecration-reading upsell.

05

Community, calendar & social

Bhaktas' homes gallery, ritual calendar with the next festival and the pieces for it, a proper live-from-the-workshop feed instead of a static grid.

06

Admin & consultant portals, redesigned

The dashboards you and the consultant live in every day, carried through to the same visual bar. Not "admin gray."
