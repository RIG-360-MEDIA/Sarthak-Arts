The Design System.

One source of truth for the whole platform. Every colour, every type size, every motion primitive, every icon, every component in every state. Nothing built until this is locked in.

Chapter 01

Design philosophy.

The visual world of Sarthak Arts is a temple you enter — sanctum-indigo ceilings, warm parchment alcoves where the pieces sit, gold as the light. Motion is measured, symbolism is real, restraint is a discipline.

Sacred, not decorative

Every symbol carries meaning in the devotional world it serves — nothing is used as ornament for its own sake.

Direction-first

The Vastu direction wheel is the primary way to explore. Categories and materials are secondary lenses.

Measured motion

One quiet gesture per section — a mandala breathes, a flame flickers, an Om glows. Never more, never restless.

Every state, considered

Default, hover, active, loading, empty, error. Every component is drawn in every state. Nothing is left to chance.

Chapter 10

Accessibility & responsive.

A devotional space that respects everyone who enters — reduced motion, focus visibility, contrast, keyboard navigation, and mobile-first (70% of Indian shoppers browse on mobile).

Focus states

Every interactive element has a visible gold focus ring (3px, .2 opacity halo). Never removed, never invisible on gold buttons.

Contrast AA / AAA

Body text meets WCAG AA. Silk on Sanctum tests 12.4:1. Ink on Ivory tests 15.2:1. Sacred accents used for structure, not for critical information alone.

Reduced motion

Every animation collapses at prefers-reduced-motion. The Om stops breathing, the mandala stands still. Ambient life becomes stillness.

Mobile-first

Designed for 375-width first (mobile Chrome / Safari). Desktop is the reward, not the default.
