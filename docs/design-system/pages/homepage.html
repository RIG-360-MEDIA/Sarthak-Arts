Sources & verification

Every element, sourced.

The design elements on this platform are not decoration — they are references to real tradition. If we display it, we can source it. This is the ledger of that discipline.

/verification
Śrī Yantra (hero)
The sovereign yantra of Śrī Lalitā Tripura Sundarī. Nine interlocking triangles (four upward = Śiva, five downward = Śakti), 8-petal Aṣṭadala lotus, 16-petal Ṣoḍaśadala lotus, three concentric circles, and the Bhūpura square with four gates. The central Bindu represents pure consciousness. Source: Śaubhāgya Bhāskara commentary on Lalitā Sahasranāma; geometric construction per Nityāṣoḍaśikārṇava.
Panchang (live data)
Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana, Choghadiya, Rahu Kaal, Sunrise/Sunset — all computed server-side from the visitor's location. Source: Swiss Ephemeris (derived from NASA JPL DE431, arcsecond precision) using Chitrapaksha (Lahiri) Ayanamsa — the Government of India Rāṣṭrīya Pañcāṅga standard. Cross-verified against Drik Panchang.
Aṣṭadikpālakas (direction shrine)
The eight directional guardians in their traditionally-fixed positions: Kubera (N · wealth · water), Īśāna (NE · water), Indra (E · space), Agni (SE · fire), Yama (S · earth), Nirṛti (SW · earth), Varuṇa (W · water), Vāyu (NW · air), with Brahmā at the Brahmasthan center (ether). Source: Bṛhat Saṃhitā (Varāhamihira), Ch. 53 · Mayamata · Mānasāra · Purāṇic tradition.
Mantras (product cards)
Each mantra is the traditional short-form for its associated deity — ॐ कुबेराय नमः for Kubera pieces, ॐ श्री महालक्ष्म्यै नमः for Lakṣmī pieces (from Lakṣmī Aṣṭottara Śatanāma), ॐ ब्रह्मणे नमः for the Brahmasthan pyramid. Source: Sahasranāma stotras and Purāṇic invocation forms. Devanagari orthography verified per Aṣṭādhyāyī Saṃhitā-pāṭha conventions.
Consecration ritual (4 steps)
Saṃkalpa → Cleansing (Śuddhi) → Placement (Sthāpanā) → First Puja (Pratiṣṭhā). The four-fold sequence traditional to Prāṇa Pratiṣṭhā — the establishment of divine presence in a consecrated object. Source: Nārada Pañcarātra Saṃhitā · Āgama-based ritual manuals. The specific mantras and gestures per piece are curated by our resident Vāstu consultant.
Festival calendar
Festival dates computed live from tithi + nakshatra rules and cross-verified against Drik Panchang — the authoritative Hindu almanac used by traditional priests. Regional variations (Amāntā vs. Pūrṇimāntā lunar month conventions) are handled by config. When our computation disagrees with Drik, we defer to Drik and note the tradition.
Material composition
Every metal weight and gemstone specification stated on a piece is measured per piece, not per SKU. The certificate that ships with every order is signed by our metallurgist. Third-party assay available on request for pieces above ₹50,000. Sourcing artisan family and workshop location is documented on every product page.

ॐ Homepage v3 · scroll-linked magic + fixes

Now it moves with you.

v3 fixes the two issues you flagged and folds in scroll-linked motion inspired by Awwwards-caliber sites, tuned to our devotional register — measured, meaningful, never restless.

What changed from v2 → v3

Fix 1 · Panchang mobile — was a broken 2-column grid with orphaned cells. Now a clean single-column list: 28px icon · 90px label · flush-right value. Now cell gets a subtle gold left-bar accent; Rahu Kaal gets a muted sindoor bar. Reads like a sacred almanac page, not a broken form.

Fix 2 · Direction shrine tilt — the constant 360° rotation was disorienting. Removed. Now the wheel sits flat by default, tilts to a temple-ceiling perspective only as you scroll into the section, and flattens again as you scroll past. On hover it tilts a further 18° — a gentle interactive gesture. Feels like entering the sanctum, not spinning inside it.

New · Golden scroll thread — a 2px gold-lit line at the very top of the viewport grows left-to-right as you scroll. Subtle. Signals the journey through the temple.

New · Yantra bloom — the Śrī Yantra now uses animation-timeline: view(). As the hero enters view, the whole yantra scales from .3 to 1 while fading in from 0 to .22 opacity — it constructs itself into being, echoing the traditional meditative construction of the yantra layer by layer.

New · Meditative Y-axis rotation — replaced the yantra's original 60s X-rotation with a much slower 240s (4-minute) Y-rotation at a subtle 12° base tilt. Feels like watching a temple ceiling breathe. Never restless.

Infrastructure · CSS scroll-timeline — the whole page is now instrumented with native CSS animation-timeline: view() and scroll(root), with JS fallback for older browsers. Ready for the next round: parallax layers, section-cross golden shimmers, product-card altar placement, ritual scroll auto-unfurl, cursor light in hero.

Scroll magic queued for v4 (research complete)

Studied the Awwwards SOTD scroll techniques — Immersive Garden, Studio Frantz, Locomotive, Ueno. Their common vocabulary: scroll-tied camera moves, layer parallax (bg/mid/fg), reveal-on-scroll with staggered delays, cursor lights, and section-boundary transitions. Fitted to our devotional register:

  • ◆ Layer-by-layer Śrī Yantra construction — Bindu → 4 Śiva triangles → 5 Śakti → Aṣṭadala → Ṣoḍaśadala → Bhūpura, keyed to scroll depth. The yantra is drawn onto you as you enter.
  • ◆ Cursor light in the sanctum — a soft warm glow follows your cursor in the hero, revealing detail.
  • ◆ Parallax background layers — yantra at 0.3× scroll speed, diyas at 0.7×, foreground at 1×.
  • ◆ Product-card altar placement — each card enters with a slight Z-rotation as if placed on the altar.
