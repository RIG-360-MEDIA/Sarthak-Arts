ॐ Phase 1 · Homepage

The homepage, section by section.

Fifteen sections designed at the same bar as the Design System — every one shown at desktop and mobile side-by-side, using the tokens, motion, and components we locked in.

How to read

Each section shows the actual rendered design at two widths — desktop (fluid) and mobile (380px). Both are live, container-queried, animating.

What's under the hood

Every colour, animation, icon and component is from Design System v1. No new tokens introduced.

Notes below each

The design decisions and mobile adaptations for each section are called out in the gold-lined notes.

  • Golden pulse dot uses pulse-dot primitive from the system to signal live.

Design decisions
  • Ambient life, not restless — five sacred animations running at once (mandala breath, two flames, particles, petals, smoke) but each so slow it's felt not seen.
  • The i in "intention" uses italic + gold-lit with a soft text-shadow glow — the sacred word receives sacred emphasis.
  • Sanskrit mantra — a single line of Devanagari sits below the lead as a benediction. Never translated inline — it lives in its own space.
  • Mobile — mandala is smaller, particles reduced from 5 to 2, petals removed entirely (motion budget). Diyas shrink but stay.
  • CTAs — primary is the golden shimmer (enter the shrine); secondary is the outlined audit trigger.

Design decisions
  • Real yantra geometry — interlocking triangles inside the wheel echo the Sri Yantra tradition, not decorative fluff.
  • Deities named at each direction — Kubera (N), Indra (E), Yama (S), Varuna (W), plus the four sub-directions. Reveals purpose.
  • The Om at center breathes on the sacred pulse — the visual heart of the whole page.
  • Mobile — smaller labels, N/S/E/W abbreviated, deity names hide until a direction is tapped (bottom-sheet reveals them).
  • Interaction — hovering a direction highlights its arc and shows a floating label with the deity, element, and one product silhouette.

Design decisions
  • Material atmosphere — each card's background gradient matches the actual metal. Copper is warm-orange, silver is cool-neutral, brass is warm-gold. The pieces feel like they're in their true light.
  • Gold arch frame — every product arch has a real gold border echoing the temple archway motif from the ornament library.
  • Deity name above product name — the small caps line ("Kubera Kalash") anchors the piece devotionally before the SKU name.
  • Sanskrit mantra on price row — quiet devotional finish. Every piece has its associated mantra shown at the bottom of the card.
  • Mobile — shows 2 of 3 (the mobile viewport reveals more via scroll). No horizontal scroll — pure vertical stack.

Design decisions
