Background worlds · live mockups

Five worlds. One decision.

Each stage below is a live, animated approximation of the world's palette, material, and motion character — with the hero copy on top so you can judge mood and readability. The interaction stack (cursor light, scroll camera, fades) carries over to whichever world you choose.

It says

"Sacred objects, forged and engraved." Material-first — the register of Cartier, P-TAL, heritage metalwork. Unmistakably a product house.

The magic

The cursor becomes the raking light — engraving gleams as your hand passes. Scroll sweeps the sheen. Same yantra, same meaning, now something you could touch.

What survives

~80% of the build — same line geometry, same cursor light, same camera and fades. Palette + material shaders change; starfield replaced by sparse workshop motes.

Effort

Small. 1–2 focused passes. The recommended path.

It says

"Craft born in fire." Visceral, warm, artisan-forward. The Thathera story made atmospheric.

The magic

Cursor stirs the sparks; scroll carries you up and out of the fire toward the finished pieces. Very alive.

Risk

Fire reads intense — less serene than a devotional brand may want site-wide. Could suit the Our-Craft page better than the homepage.

Effort

Medium. New particle behavior + palette; yantra would need a supporting, not central, role.

It says

"A heritage house with a sanctum." Architectural, devotional, serene — temple as place, not symbol. The strongest 'walk inside' narrative.

The magic

Scroll walks you through the arches — real parallax depth, camera passing gateway after gateway. Cursor is the diya you carry, lighting what it nears.

Risk

The most ambitious: real arch geometry needs careful modeling to avoid looking like clip-art. Biggest chance of 'clumsy' if rushed.

Effort

Large. A proper multi-day build with new 3D architecture assets.

Which world does Sarthak Arts live in?

My honest read: A — Engraved Metal is the brand. It keeps the yantra and its meaning, keeps everything we built, and moves the story from "we read the stars" to "we forge the sacred." B and D are beautiful side-rooms (Our Craft, gifting pages). C is the grandest — worth it later, with real assets. 0 stays a legitimate choice if you love the cosmos.
