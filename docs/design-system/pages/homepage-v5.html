On the making

The seven days a copper
kalash takes to make.

A single sheet of copper, cut to a disc. Bhavesh sets it on the anvil and begins — four thousand hammer strikes, over seven days, the metal rising into a vessel by the will of his hand alone. No mould. No press. The vessel remembers every blow, and every blow is different.

This is not a production method. It is a discipline that Bhavesh's grandfather taught his father, and that his father taught him. The Punjab Thatheras were listed by UNESCO in 2014 for this reason — the technique itself is heritage. We do not modify it. We do not speed it up. We pay for the days it takes.

Which is why you cannot find a Sarthak Arts kalash at a discount. There is no wholesale version. There is no faster batch. There is one artisan, one week, one vessel — and then the certificate that says so, in his hand.

The colophon.

Everything on this page is sourced. If we display it, we can name where it comes from. This is the discipline of the trade, and it is also the promise.

Śrī Yantra
The sovereign yantra of Śrī Lalitā Tripura Sundarī. Geometry per Nityāṣoḍaśikārṇava and the Śaubhāgya Bhāskara commentary. Nine interlocking triangles (four Śiva upward, five Śakti downward), eight-petal Aṣṭadala lotus, sixteen-petal Ṣoḍaśadala lotus, three concentric circles, and the Bhūpura square with four gates.
Panchang
Tithi, Nakṣatra, Yoga, Karaṇa, Choghadiya, Rāhu Kāla, sunrise and sunset — all computed server-side from your location using the Swiss Ephemeris (derived from NASA JPL DE431, arcsecond precision) with Chitrapaksha (Lahiri) Ayanamsa, the standard of the Rāṣṭrīya Pañcāṅga. Cross-verified daily against Drik Panchang.
Aṣṭadikpālakas
The eight directional guardians in their traditionally-fixed positions per Bṛhat Saṃhitā, Ch. 53 · Mayamata · Mānasāra · and Purāṇic tradition. Elements per the Pañcamahābhūta framework.
Mantras
Each mantra shown for a piece is the traditional short-form invocation of its associated deity — from the Sahasranāma stotras and Purāṇic invocation forms. Devanagari orthography verified per Aṣṭādhyāyī Saṃhitā-pāṭha conventions.
The four-step ritual
Saṃkalpa · Śuddhi · Sthāpanā · Pratiṣṭhā. The traditional sequence of Prāṇa Pratiṣṭhā — the establishment of divine presence in a consecrated object. Sources: Nārada Pañcarātra Saṃhitā and Āgama-based ritual manuals. Per-piece mantras and gestures curated by our resident Vāstu consultant.
Materials
Every metal weight and gemstone specification is measured per piece, not per SKU. The certificate that ships with every order carries our metallurgist's signature. Third-party assay available on request for any piece above ₹50,000.
