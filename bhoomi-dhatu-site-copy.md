## 7. "Our craft" page

**Headline:** Made by hand, not run through a mould twice.

**Intro:**
Every piece in this collection passes through the hands of one metalworker from raw sheet or ingot to finished object. We work with a small circle of coppersmiths, silversmiths and stone-setters, most trained in family workshops that have done this for generations. We don't run production batches — we run orders.

**Section: The materials**
We use copper, brass and silver at stated purity, and gold only as a thin overlay where a listing says so — we don't inflate a brass piece into something it isn't. Every product page lists the exact metal weight, not a range. If a listing says 420 grams of copper, that's what's on your shelf.

**Section: The stones**
Gemstones are chosen by hand for color and clarity, not by carat alone. Each stone is set, not glued, so it can be reset or replaced by a jeweller decades from now if needed.

**Section: The process**
1. **Design** — Every piece starts from a specific direction and purpose, not a general "Vastu item" brief.
2. **Shape** — Metal is cut, raised or cast by hand, then finished with traditional hammer or engraving work.
3. **Set** — The gemstone is set once the metal piece is complete and cooled.
4. **Certify** — Each piece is weighed and its exact composition recorded before it ships.

## 8. "The philosophy" / Vastu education section

**Headline:** Vastu Shastra, briefly

**Body:**
Vastu Shastra is a traditional Indian system for arranging buildings and objects so that a home works with natural energy rather than against it — light, air, water, and the placement of rooms and objects relative to the eight compass directions. It's often compared to Feng Shui, though the two developed independently and follow different principles.

We're not going to claim our products guarantee an outcome — what we can tell you is that every piece we sell is built to the traditional specification for its direction and purpose: right metal, right size, right placement. What you do with it in your home is yours to decide.

**Sub-section: Do I need a consultation?**
If your home follows a fairly standard layout, the direction guide on each product page is usually enough. If your layout is unusual — an L-shaped plot, a home with mixed-use floors, a corner apartment — a short consultation can save you from placing a piece somewhere it won't do much.

## 9. Trust / FAQ section

**Headline:** Common questions

**Is this real gold/silver, or plated?**
Each listing states this exactly. Where we use a gold overlay on brass, we say "gold-accent" or "gold overlay," never "gold," in the product name. Solid silver and copper pieces are stated as solid.

**Why do prices change?**
Our prices track the daily cost of the metal used in each piece, so what you pay reflects what the material is worth, not an old price list. The price shown at checkout is locked in for your order.

**Do you offer a certificate of authenticity?**
Yes — every order ships with a certificate stating the exact metal weight and gemstone in your piece.

**What if I'm not sure which direction applies to my home?**
Use the direction guide, or book a short consultation — we'll ask for your home's orientation and layout and point you to the right pieces.

**Can copper darken over time?**
Yes, and that's expected — copper develops a natural patina with air exposure, which in Vastu tradition is not considered a flaw. If you prefer to keep the bright finish, a light polish restores it.
